Easy 3D Export — Chrome installation

1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep, such as Easy 3D Export.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
5. Pin Easy 3D Export and click its icon to open the side panel.
6. Open a Meshy or Hyper3D Rodin model page and refresh it to detect the model.

No Node.js, pnpm, or build step is needed for this release ZIP.
Keep the extracted folder in place: Chrome loads the extension from that folder.

Updates: replace the folder contents with the new release, click Reload on the
extension in chrome://extensions, and refresh your provider tabs.
